Grading rules Provisional

GradeRuleWhere it goes
ASOH ≥ 70% and resistance rise ≤ 50%, no damageCold storage and clinics
BSOH 50 to under 70%, or resistance rise 50 to 100%Kiosk power and lighting
RejectSOH under 50%, resistance rise over 100%, peak temperature over 60°C, or any swelling or leakRecycler
Sources and assumptions
  • Retired mobility packs are commonly pulled near 80% state of health or a 200% resistance rise, and fade turns non-linear in the 80 to 70% zone (TWAICE end-of-life guide). Cutoffs in this demo are adapted to that and are provisional until calibrated on field data.
  • A three-tier split around 80% and 50% state of health is widely used, with exact cutoffs varying by organization (arXiv 2512.01294 review of retired lithium-ion diagnostics).
  • Backup hours = 2.0 kWh rated energy x SOH x 80% depth of discharge x 90% inverter efficiency, divided by the load. The 2 kWh figure matches the older Spiro Commando pack. Depth of discharge, efficiency and the 60°C limit are assumptions.
  • The 21 W default load follows Kenyan off-grid small-business averages of 13 to 21 W (Dauenhauer and Louie, 2015). A cold room needs a much larger load; enter it above.
  • IEC 62619:2022 covers first-life batteries and leaves reuse out of scope, so no second-life safety standard exists yet. This is the gap the passport and protocol address.
  • The site map is schematic. Site names and positions are illustrative, not real deployments.
